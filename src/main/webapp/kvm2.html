Karnataka 5 Day Package

Karnataka, India

Duration: 5 Days / 5 Nights

Hotels:

  • Trivik Hotels & Resorts: A luxury resort offering panoramic views of the Western Ghats, known for its exceptional service and amenities.
  • The Serai Chikmagalur: A premium resort set amidst coffee plantations, offering a blend of luxury and nature.
  • Coffee Grove Resort: A mid-range resort surrounded by coffee estates, ideal for families and nature lovers.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Mullayanagiri
  • Day 2:Baba Budangiri
  • Day 3: Jhari Falls
  • Day 4: Veera Narayana Temple, Belavadi
  • Day 5:Annapoorneshwari Temple, Horanadu

Local Food:

  • Halasinakayi Happala
  • Ellu Holige (Sesame Flatbread)
  • Chigli Unde
  • Kesari Bath

Price: ₹8,000 per person
