Kerala 4 Day Package

Kerala,India

Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights

Hotels:

  • Trivik Hotels & Resorts: A luxury resort offering panoramic views of the Western Ghats, known for its exceptional service and amenities.
  • The Serai Chikmagalur: A premium resort set amidst coffee plantations, offering a blend of luxury and nature.
  • Gateway Chikmagalur – IHCL SeleQtions: A heritage hotel offering comfortable stays with modern amenities.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Arrive in Chikmagalur and check into your hotel. Visit Mullayanagiri and enjoy trekking to the summit.
  • Day 2: Head to Kemmangundi and visit Hebbe Falls.
  • Day 3:Visit the Veera Narayana Temple in Belavadi. Explore the Annapoorneshwari Temple in Horanadu.
  • Day 4: Return to Chikmagalur and depart.

Local Food:

  • Halasinakayi Happala (Jackfruit Papad): A crispy snack made from jackfruit pulp mixed with spices and sun-dried.
  • Ellu Holige (Sesame Flatbread): A sweet flatbread filled with a mixture of sesame seeds and jaggery, traditionally prepared during festivals.
  • Chigli Unde: A unique Malnad delicacy made from ant eggs, jaggery, and coconut, typically consumed as a sweet snack.

Price: ₹5500 per person
