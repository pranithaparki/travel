Cherai Beach

Cherai is a suburb of Kochi city, a land gifted with the splendour of nature. The whole region is surrounded by water bodies like any other part of Cochin. Cherai beach is one of the most captivating beaches in Kerala with calm and quiet atmosphere. This beach is mostly less crowded, presenting a cosy atmosphere. Every year, travellers from all around the world visit Cherai Beach to experience the peace and calmness, away from the busy city life.

Jog Falls

Jog Falls, a major attraction in Karnataka, is located in the Shimoga district of Karnataka. Also known by alternative names of Gerusoppe Falls, Gersoppa Falls, and Jogada Gundi, it is one of the highest waterfalls in India. During monsoons, one can see a breathtaking view of the falls with rainbows coming every now and then.

Vivekananda rock memorial

Vivekananda Rock Memorial is a monumental tribute located on a small island off the coast of Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu, India. This iconic structure honors the revered philosopher and spiritual leader Swami Vivekananda, who is known for his teachings on Vedanta and his efforts to promote Hindu philosophy and spirituality.
