Kerala 3 Day Package

Kerala, India

Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights

Hotels:

  • Uday Suites – The Airport Hotel: Located close to Trivandrum International Airport, this hotel offers spacious rooms, a serene garden area, and a swimming pool. It’s ideal for spiritual tourists due to its proximity to major temples and quiet ambiance.
  • Mascot Hotel, Trivandrum: A heritage hotel managed by KTDC, located centrally and close to temples like Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple. It features classic colonial architecture, comfortable rooms, and traditional Kerala cuisine.
  • Apollo Dimora, Trivandrum: A 4-star hotel known for its convenient location, elegant interiors, and excellent hospitality. It’s a popular choice among pilgrims visiting the sacred sites of Trivandrum.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1:Arrival in Trivandrum – Visit to Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, Pazhavangadi Ganapathy Temple
  • Day 2:Attukal Bhagavathy Temple, Sreekanteswaram Temple, Napier Museum (optional), local market visit
  • Day 3: Check-out, Visit to Kovalam Beach or Vellayani Lake en route departure

Local Food:

  • Kerala Sadya (with dishes like Avial, Thoran, Kalan, Payasam)
  • Idiyappam with Vegetable Stew
  • Neyyappam (Sweet rice fritter)

Price: ₹4,000 per person
