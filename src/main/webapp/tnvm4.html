Tamilnadu 3 Day Package

Tamilnadu, India

Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights

Hotels:

  • Green Nest Resort, Kotagiri: Just a few kilometers from Catherine Falls, this serene resort is surrounded by tea plantations and forested hills. It offers cozy cottages, mountain views, and peaceful nature walks—perfect for a quiet hill escape.
  • La Maison, Kotagiri: A boutique homestay with European-style interiors and stunning valley views. Ideal for couples or small families looking to relax while being close to trekking trails and waterfalls.
  • Bison Valley Estate Stay: Located in a private estate near Kotagiri, this stay offers rustic charm with modern comforts. Great for travelers wanting a quiet experience close to nature, with easy access to Catherine Falls and tea estates.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1:Arrival in Kotagiri – Tea estate walk, Sunset at Rangaswamy Peak
  • Day 2: : Morning trek to Catherine Falls – Explore the double-cascading waterfall with scenic valley views. Afternoon visit to Kodanad View Point or Dolphin’s Nose
  • Day 3:Local shopping (tea, homemade chocolates), optional visit to a tea factory, Departure

Local Food:

  • Millet Dosa with Tomato Chutney – A healthy, earthy breakfast option in the hills
  • Nilgiri Mutton Curry – Cooked with local herbs and spices, served with rice or parotta
  • South Indian Veg Meals – Freshly prepared with local vegetables and aromatic sambhar

Price: ₹5,000 per person
