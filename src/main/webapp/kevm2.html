Kerala 4 Day Package

Kerala, India

Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights

Hotels:

  • The Leaf Munnar: Nestled amidst the lush greenery of Munnar, The Leaf offers a peaceful retreat with scenic views, modern amenities, and cozy cottages. It features an outdoor pool, children’s play area, and multi-cuisine restaurant. Located about 11 km from Munnar town and close to attractions like Attukad Waterfalls, it provides a perfect blend of comfort and nature.
  • Tea County, Munnar: Managed by Kerala Tourism Development Corporation, Tea County is set in a serene environment with panoramic views of tea gardens. It offers spacious rooms, Ayurvedic spa treatments, and indoor games. Located centrally in Munnar, it gives easy access to major tourist spots.
  • Amber Dale Luxury Hotel & Spa: A 5-star property perched on a hilltop, offering luxurious rooms with valley views, fine dining, and holistic spa treatments. The hotel is around 8 km from Munnar town and close to Pothamedu View Point.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Arrival in Munnar – Check-in, Visit to Blossom Park & Pothamedu View Point
  • Day 2: Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point
  • Day 3:Tea Museum, Kundala Lake, Top Station
  • Day 4: Check-out, Visit to Spice Plantation en route departure

Local Food:

  • Kerala Sadya (Traditional vegetarian meal served on a banana leaf)
  • Malabar Parotta with Kerala Chicken Curry
  • Nadan Kozhi Varuthathu (Spicy Fried Chicken)

Price: ₹6,000 per person
