Karnataka 7 Day Package

Karnataka, India

Duration: 7 Days / 6 Nights

Hotels:

  • The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore: The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore is centrally located in the heart of Bangalore in close proximity to the finest in shopping, dining and entertainment experiences. Situated in Bangalore, close to UB City Mall, Commercial Street and Brigade Road, The Ritz - Carlton, Bangalore is a picturesque property with a 24-hour front desk and free Wi-Fi throughout the property. Filling the hotel’s entire fifth floor, The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Bangalore offers luxury treatments drawing from Indian and Eastern healing traditions, a salon and swimming pool. Twelve treatment rooms include a VIP suite and two express service rooms. The hotel is located 37 km from Bengaluru Airport and 3 km from Cantonment Railway Station. The Majestic Bus Stand is 5 km away. The famous Lal Bagh is 1.5 km away and the Bull Temple is 5 km away.
  • Radisson Blu Plaza, Mysore: Radisson Blu is an international chain of upscale hotels. Its hotels are mainly located in major cities, key airport gateways and leisure destinations. Radisson Blu has roots dating back to the opening of the SAS Royal Hotel in Denmark in 1960 and was the world's first designer hotel.
  • The Windflower Resorts, Coorg: The Windflower Resorts & Spa is widely appreciated for its serene ambiance and a variety of amenities that contribute to a relaxing getaway, with guests particularly enjoying the recreational facilities and diverse dining options. Despite the spacious rooms set amidst lush greenery, some guests feel that upkeep and the provision of basic amenities fall short. The staff's warmth and helpfulness stand out, though occasional service inconsistencies are noted.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Bangalore – Lalbagh Botanical Garden, UB City
  • Day 2: Murudeshwar – Temple Visit, Beach Activities
  • Day 3: Mahabaleshwar Temple, Gokarna Beach
  • Day 4: Abbey Falls & Coffee Plantations, Coorg
  • Day 5: Chikmagalur – Mullayanagiri Trek, Coffee Museum

Local Food:

  • Ragi Mudde with Sambar
  • Bisi Bele Bath, Mysore Pak
  • Kori Rotti (Chicken Curry with Crispy Roti)
  • Filter Coffee, Neer Dosa
