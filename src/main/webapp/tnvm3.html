Tamilnadu 4 Day Package

tamilnadu, India

Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights

Hotels:

  • The Carlton, Kodaikanal: Overlooking the serene Kodaikanal Lake, The Carlton is a premium heritage hotel offering colonial charm and modern comforts. The hotel features lake-view rooms, landscaped gardens, and fine dining. It’s perfect for guests looking to unwind in luxury while staying close to major sightseeing points.
  • Sterling Kodai Lake: Set amidst pine groves near the lake, Sterling Kodai Lake offers cozy cottages and a peaceful ambiance. The resort has activities like bonfires, nature walks, and indoor games, making it ideal for families and honeymooners.
  • Kodai Resort Hotel: Just a short walk from Coaker’s Walk and Bryant Park, this property provides independent cottages surrounded by lush greenery. The in-house restaurant serves South Indian and continental food, and the views of the valley are a highlight.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1:Arrival – Kodaikanal Lake boating, Coaker’s Walk, Bryant Park
  • Day 2:Pillar Rocks, Guna Caves, Pine Forest, Moir Point
  • Day 3: Silver Cascade Falls, Kurinji Andavar Temple, Shenbaganur Museum of Natural History
  • Day 4:: Early morning walk, local shopping (homemade chocolates, essential oils), departure

Local Food:

  • Hot Bajji with Tea – A must-try on misty evenings near viewpoints.
  • South Indian Breakfast – Pongal, Upma, and crispy Dosas with chutneys.
  • Vegetable Kurma with Appam – Light, spicy, and served in most local restaurants.

Price: ₹7,000 per person
