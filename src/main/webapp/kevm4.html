Kerala 3 Day Package

Kerala, India

Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights

Hotels:

  • Kumarakom Lake Resort: One of Kerala’s most luxurious heritage resorts, located on the serene Vembanad Lake. Known for traditional architecture, infinity pools, Ayurveda centre, and houseboat stays. The resort offers heritage villas with private pools and lake views, ideal for a relaxing escape.
  • The Zuri Kumarakom: A 5-star deluxe resort offering lagoon-facing rooms, a world-class spa, multiple dining options, and a peaceful atmosphere. The resort is well-suited for luxury travelers looking to experience Kerala’s backwaters with premium comfort.
  • Taj Kumarakom Resort & Spa: A charming colonial-style retreat surrounded by lush greenery and backwaters. With personalized service, gourmet dining, and an Ayurvedic spa, this hotel is perfect for an intimate luxury getaway.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Arrival in Kumarakom – Check-in, Backwater Sunset Cruise
  • Day 2:Visit to Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary, Traditional Kerala Massage, Leisure time at resort
  • Day 3:Check-out, Optional visit to a nearby village or Alleppey for houseboat ride en route departure

Local Food:

  • Karimeen Pollichathu (Pearl spot fish cooked in banana leaf)
  • Chemmeen Curry (Prawn Curry in coconut gravy) •
  • Elaneer Payasam (Tender coconut dessert)

Price: ₹3500 per person
