Tamilnadu 4 Day Package

Tamilnadu, India

Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights

Hotels:

  • Hotels: • The Residency, T. Nagar: The Residency is situated in the heart of Chennai’s busiest shopping district, T. Nagar. It offers a balance of modern amenities and warm South Indian hospitality. Guests enjoy quick access to iconic heritage spots, traditional markets, and cultural centers. The in-house restaurant serves a wide range of Tamil delicacies along with global cuisine.
  • The Raintree, Anna Salai: This eco-conscious hotel is known for its sleek architecture and rooftop dining with panoramic views of the city. Located on Anna Salai, it’s close to the Government Museum, Fort St. George, and other colonial-era attractions. The Raintree’s comfortable rooms, friendly service, and focus on sustainability make it a favorite among heritage travelers.
  • Taj Connemara: Taj Connemara is Chennai’s oldest operating hotel, with a legacy that dates back to the British Raj. Its colonial-style architecture, antique decor, and serene courtyards take guests on a historical journey. The hotel offers curated heritage experiences, fine South Indian dining, and proximity to Marina Beach and Spencer Plaza.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Arrival – Kapaleeshwarar Temple, San Thome Basilica, Marina beach
  • Day 2:Government Museum, Fort St. George, Vivekananda House
  • Day 3:DakshinaChitra, Kalakshetra Foundation, Theosophical Society
  • Day 4: Local Shopping – Spencer Plaza, Departure

Local Food:

  • Idli with Sambar & Coconut Chutney – A soft rice cake breakfast staple.
  • Chettinad Chicken Curry – Fiery, aromatic curry from the Chettinad region.
  • Tamil Vegetarian Thali – A balanced platter with sambhar, rasam, poriyal, appalam, and rice.
