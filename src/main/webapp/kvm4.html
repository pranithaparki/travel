Karnataka 5 Day Package

Karnataka, India

Duration: 7 Nights / 8 Days

Hotels:

  • Luxury Resorts Evolve Back, Coorg (Chikkana Halli Estate) Set amidst a 300-acre coffee plantation, this resort offers luxurious villas with private pools, blending traditional Kodava architecture with modern amenities.
  • Taj Madikeri Resort & Spa Perched on a hillside, this resort provides panoramic views of the rainforest, offering spacious rooms, an infinity pool, and a holistic spa experience.
  • Coorg Marriott Resort & Spa A 5-star property nestled amidst lush landscapes, it offers contemporary rooms, a full-service spa, and multiple dining options.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1:Abbey Falls
  • Day 2:Mandalpatti Peak
  • Day 3:Dubare Elephant Camp
  • Day 4: Chiklihole Reservoir
  • Day 5:Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple)

Local Food:

  • Kadambuttu (Steamed Rice Dumplings)
  • Baimbale Curry
  • Noolputtu
  • Akki Roti

Price: ₹12,000 per person
