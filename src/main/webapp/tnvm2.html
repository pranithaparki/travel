Tamilnadu 3 Day Package

Tamilnadu, India

Duration: 7 Days / 6 Nights

Hotels:

  • Fortune Pandiyan Hotel: A well-known heritage-inspired hotel offering a blend of modern comfort and traditional décor. Located close to the Meenakshi Temple, it offers spacious rooms, garden views, and authentic Tamil cuisine.
  • Astoria Hotels by Sparsa: Located in the heart of Madurai, this modern boutique hotel offers easy access to the temple and city markets. It features stylish rooms, friendly service, and local food specialties.
  • Royal Court: Just a few minutes from the Meenakshi Amman Temple and railway station, Royal Court offers convenient, comfortable lodging with a focus on heritage hospitality and vegetarian cuisine.

Itinerary:

  • Day 1:Arrival – Visit to Meenakshi Amman Temple (evening darshan), explore Pudhu Mandapam and local night markets
  • Day 2:Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace, Koodal Azhagar Temple, Gandhi Memorial Museum. Optional: Light and Sound Show at the palace
  • Day 3:Morning visit to Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple, local shopping for handicrafts and Madurai cotton sarees – Departure

Local Food:

  • Idiyappam with Coconut Milk – A soft, steamed rice noodle dish served for breakfast or dinner
  • Madurai Kari Dosai – A three-layer dosa topped with spicy minced mutton, unique to the region
  • Sambhar Vadai and Filter Coffee – A classic temple-town snack • Madurai Jigarthanda – A famous chilled dessert made with milk, almond gum, and ice cream

Price: ₹5,000 per person
